### An Improved Visual Enhancement Method for Color Images

ABSTRACT
The paper presents an improved method for color image enhancement based on dynamic range compression and shadow compensation method proposed by Felix Albu et al. The improved method consists of not only the dynamic range compression and shadow compensation but also intensity contrast stretching implemented under the logarithmic image processing (LIP) model. The previous method enhance image while preserve image details and color information without generating visual artifacts. On the premise of remaining the advantages of the previous one, our improved method enhances the intensity of the whole image especially the low-light areas. The experimental results illustrate the effectiveness of our proposed method and the superiority over the previous one.
Keywords:Color image enhancement, Logarithmic image processing, Dynamic range compression, Shadow compensation, Intensity contrast stretching
1. INTRODUCTION
Color image, which has been widely used in many areas such as medical imaging and aerial photograph, contain much more information than gray-scale images. Therefore, the enhancement of color image, which aims to improve the image quality for human visual perception, is of great significant in image analysis and process. The most popular method for gray-scale image enhancement is histogram equalization [1], which is based on the assumption that a uniformly distributed gray-scale histogram will have the best visual contrast. With the concept of histogram equalization, Trahanias and Venetsanopoulos [2] proposed a uniform 3-D histogram specification method in the RGB color space. Felix Albu and Constantin Vertan [3] proposed a technique of dynamic range compression and shadow compensation for color images. It works well on enhancing image preserving the colors and details. To achieve the algorithms, color image enhancement is formulated as an optimization problem.
The existing methods mentioned above have several drawbacks. The uniform 3-D histogram specification method in the RGB color space didn't take the physical image formation model and the human visual system into consideration. The dynamic range compression and shadow compensation method was not effective enough in dark areas. The logarithmic image processing model proposed by Jourlin and Pinoli [4-7] provides a mathematical framework which is compatible with the physical image formation model and human visual system. Therefore, we extend the LIP model in RGB color space and improve the dynamic range compression and shadow compensation method combining intensity contrast stretching method applied in the RGB color space. The results obtained from the improved method are compared with that obtained form the traditional one and the uniform 3-D histogram specification method.
The reminder of the paper is organized as follow: Relatated works are described in Section 2. Our proposed method based on LIP model is proposed in Section 3. The experimental results are illustrated in Section 4 and the conclusion is given in Section 5.

According to Felix Albu and Constantin Vertan [3], the dynamic range compression and shadow compensation method enhance the images with adaptive, pixel-specific color component amplification factors that are computed according to the local contrast and pre-computed setup parameters. The original global contrast is increased in the case of low-contrast, shadowed images and kept at near constant values in the remainder of the cases. The amplification is performed with a non-linear, perceptual-resembling logarithmic multiplication. And by using a recursive filter, the method can be implemented as a one-pass image processing operation. In addition, it is very suitable for implementation on low power, low memory embedded devices for using local image information and processing the image in a single scan.

5. CONCLUSION
This paper presents an improved method for color image enhancement. The proposed method improved the dynamic range compression and shadow compensation method by combining the intensity contrast stretching method which is applied under the LIP model. According to the simulation results obtained from the experiments, our improved enhancement method can enhance all areas of image especially the low-light areas, while at the same time preserving image details and color information without generating visual artifacts, which is suitable for human visual perception.
